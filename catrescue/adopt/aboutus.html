About Whisker Haven

Founded in 1999 Whisker Haven started as a small group of friends wanting to help with the abandoned cats around their neighborhood.
Their goals from the very beginning were to provide care and hope to abandoned cats and to give them a loving life. Over the years, we have
expanded and helped out thousands of cats in the process.
